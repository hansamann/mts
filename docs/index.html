For real?

Why meet in a sauna?

It's about creating a space for genuine, meaningful and honest conversations, where status, titles, and appearances matter less.

What do people talk about?

Everything from AI, startups, and software to books, science, travel, careers, and life. There are no presentations or pitches, just conversations.

What is the dress code?

You're welcome to wrap yourself in a towel and go at your own pace. We follow German sauna etiquette: no swimsuits inside the sauna. Please bring two large towels, one to wrap yourself and one to sit on, so no skin touches the wood.

Do I have to work in tech?

Not at all. We welcome anyone who prefers thoughtful conversations about technology, startups, science, or interesting ideas.

Cost?

Just buy a 4-hour sauna ticket at the entrance. We usually meet directly inside the sauna area. Don't worry if you are late, you will find us inside.

Is it safe for women?

Absolutely. Women are a regular part of our meetups, and we make sure everyone to feel comfortable and welcomed. Any inappropriate behavior, or unwanted attention is not tolerated.

Munich's most unique meetup

What to bring?

Slippers, 2-3 towels, some cash, and a water bottle. One towel is for sitting or lying on in the sauna to keep the benches clean and comfortable.

Bonus points if you rock a classic sauna hat to keep your head cool and your style hot.
